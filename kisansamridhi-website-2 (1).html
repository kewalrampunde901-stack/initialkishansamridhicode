About Us

Farmers First,
Business Second

KisanSamridhi is a mission-driven agricultural LLP founded in Chhindwara, Madhya Pradesh — expanding its reach across the entire state through a strong franchise network.

🌱

Quality Agricultural Inputs

Seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides — all sourced from certified and trusted brands.

🤝

Integrated Distribution Network

From our mother store to 10 franchise outlets — a seamless supply chain across Madhya Pradesh.

📚

Expert Agricultural Guidance

MSc Agriculture qualified team providing complete support — from field to market.

"Prosperity in every field, abundance in every home."

Our vision is to ensure that every farmer in Madhya Pradesh has access to quality agricultural inputs — at the right time, at the right price. KisanSamridhi LLP is bringing this vision to life through a strong, transparent, and farmer-first business model.

Our Leadership

The People Behind KisanSamridhi

Our leadership team brings deep expertise in agriculture — from ground-level field operations to strategic business development.

JC
Jitendra Choure
Founder & Director

Mr. Jitendra Choure is the founding visionary behind KisanSamridhi. He laid the foundation of the company with a clear mission: to build an integrated, trustworthy agricultural service network for the farmers of Madhya Pradesh.

His leadership has driven the company's growth from Chhindwara into a multi-district franchise presence across MP.

🌾 Visionary Founder 📍 Chhindwara, MP
KP
Kewalram Punde
Chief Executive Officer

Mr. Kewalram Punde holds an MSc in Agriculture (Livestock Production Management) and brings extensive field experience from ITC Mission Sunehra Kal, FPO coordination, and agri-input sales across multiple districts.

His hands-on experience across Betul, Chhindwara, and Hoshangabad districts directly shapes the company's operational and growth strategy.

🎓 MSc Agriculture 🏆 ITC Alumni
